# Solstime

Solstime is a 24-hour planner that represents the day as a solar dial.

## Product

- The dial places noon at the top and midnight at the bottom.
- Sunrise and sunset are calculated dynamically for the configured location.
- Daylight and night are represented as distinct regions on the dial.
- The current local time is shown at the center.

## Technology

- Next.js App Router
- TypeScript
- Atomic Design component structure
- Solar calculations are performed locally in the browser.
